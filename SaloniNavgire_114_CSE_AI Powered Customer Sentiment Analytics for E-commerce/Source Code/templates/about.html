About SentimentPulse

SentimentPulse is built to make customer feedback easier to understand. We turn product reviews into an actionable sentiment overview, so teams can identify what’s working and what needs improvement — fast.

You can try the product-link analyzer in guest mode. After your free trials are used, you can create an account and log in to continue using all features without limits.

Back to home
Mission
Empower teams with a clean, fast way to interpret customer reviews and make better product decisions.
What we do
Extract reviews from supported product pages and classify them into Positive / Neutral / Negative with confidence.
Why it matters
Review sentiment highlights common pain points and strengths — helping prioritize fixes, messaging, and product improvements.
